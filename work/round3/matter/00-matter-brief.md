# Matter Brief

## Task

This folder is the working file for the AutoClaw Round 3 competition entry (Legal Practice track, November 5-20, 2026). The goal is a complete, verifiable contract-review workflow rather than a narrative post.

## Fictional fact pattern

- **Customer:** Northwind Precision Manufacturing, Inc., an Ohio corporation, roughly 400 employees, three plants.
- **Provider:** Skyline Device Cloud, Inc., a Delaware corporation headquartered in Austin, Texas.
- **Deal:** 36-month subscription to a device-telemetry platform, 250 named users, US$180,000 per year.
- **Posture:** Customer received Provider's standard form and needs a signature-ready redline.

All parties, fees, and clause language are fictional. No real customer, contract, or personal data appears anywhere in this folder.
