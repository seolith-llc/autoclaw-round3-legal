# House Style and Terminology

Applies to every document in `work/round3/`. US English throughout: no CJK characters, US spelling, US date and currency formats.

## 1. Spelling and punctuation

| Use | Not |
| --- | --- |
| organize, recognize, analyze | organise, recognise, analyse |
| license (noun), license (verb, legal drafting) | licence |
| judgment | judgement |
| Section 5.2 (capitalized when referring to this contract) | section 5.2, §5.2 |
| serial comma: "gross negligence, willful misconduct, and breach of Section 6" | no serial comma |
| em dash with spaces is avoided; use a colon or a new sentence | wide use of — |
| "US$180,000" on first mention, "$180,000" thereafter | USD 180,000 |
| "November 6, 2026" | 6 November 2026, 11/06/26 |

## 2. Party names

| Context | Use | Not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contract text and clause analysis | Customer / Provider (defined terms, capitalized) | client, vendor, you |
| General commentary and checklist | customer, vendor (lowercase) | Customer when no specific party |

## 3. Document names

| Concept | Use | Not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The provider's unmodified form | vendor draft | original, base draft |
| The tracked-changes version | redline | mark-up, red-line |
| The revised text without markup | clean revised draft | final, clean copy |
| "Red line" as a metaphor | avoid | "red-line item" |

The last row matters: "redline" names a file, "red line" used metaphorically confuses the two. Say "walk-away item" or "must-fix item" instead.

## 4. Statutory citation

- Full citation on first use in a document: "Cal. Civ. Code section 1798.100(d)", "UCC section 2-316", "15 U.S.C. section 8403".
- Thereafter: "section 1798.100(d)", "section 2-316".
- Do not use the section symbol in running text; use "section" and keep the number exact.
- Subdivisions use parentheses: section 1798.100(d)(1)-(5), section 17602(b)(2).
- Verify before citing. Two frequent errors this project caught: the CCPA service-provider terms are in 1798.100(d), not (e); 16 CFR Part 425 does not currently contain a general click-to-cancel requirement.

## 5. Risk vocabulary

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Critical | Do not sign until resolved; typically a statutory compliance failure |
| High | Material risk shift; must fix or price |
| Medium | Negotiable; trade against a Critical or High item |
| Walk-away | A Critical item that cannot be cured by price |
| Trade | Concede to hold a higher-priority item |

## 6. Numbers and units

- Spell out one through nine in prose; use numerals for 10 and above, and for all clause references.
- Percentages: "5%", "99.5%".
- Dates in headers: "November 6, 2026".
- Money: "US$180,000 per year".

## 7. Voice

- Active voice unless the actor is unknown.
- One idea per sentence in the memo's analysis sections.
- No hedging chains ("it may be possible that"). Say "likely", "unlikely", or "unsettled".
